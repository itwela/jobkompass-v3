# Code of Conduct

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space connected to this project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith until they show otherwise.
- Give feedback that someone can act on. Say what is wrong and what would fix it.
- Accept feedback on your own work without taking it personally.
- Stay on topic. Issues are for the project.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or jokes about who someone is
- Sexual language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Repeatedly pushing after being asked to stop

## People's resumes are private

JobKompass handles resumes and job-search data. Do not post another person's resume, contact details or application history in an issue or pull request. When you need to show a problem, use made-up data or remove the identifying details first.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who do not follow this. Decisions are made by the maintainers and are final.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainers privately. Use the same private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are kept confidential.
